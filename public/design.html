02 — About

Designer who ships the engineering.

I'm Omar Medhat, a designer-engineer based in Cairo working across UI/UX and front-end. I care about interfaces at the system level — component libraries, tokens, and the underlying data model — not just the surface of a single screen.

My approach is systems over screens. On RHB that meant one shared component library driving the parent brand and Bab Alsalam, and Figma plugin scripts that swept sitewide fixes in a single operation instead of eighty by hand. On Dar Senosi it meant a metafield-driven data contract that gave one Shopify store three different purchase flows without a custom app. Both come from the same instinct — find the underlying model, then let it drive everything downstream.

I like the hard, unglamorous parts: auto-layout that self-sizes, CTAs that swap declaratively, forms that don't hide behind the footer, handoffs a client can actually maintain. Currently taking on select design-systems and front-end work where precision under constraint matters more than volume.
