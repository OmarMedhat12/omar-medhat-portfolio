03 — About

Designer who ships the engineering.

I'm Omar Medhat, a designer-engineer based in Cairo working across UI/UX and front-end. I care about interfaces at the system level — component libraries, tokens, and the underlying data model — not just the surface of a single screen.

My approach is systems over screens. On Dar Senosi that meant a metafield-driven data contract giving one Shopify store three different purchase flows without a custom app — plus sixteen custom sections, a full editorial design system, and a live deployment the client maintains independently. Same instinct every time: find the underlying model, then let it drive everything downstream.

I work with GCC brands, MENA boutiques, Shopify stores, and product teams that need one person who can design precisely and ship the code. Currently taking on select projects where craft and constraint matter more than volume — storefronts, design systems, and front-end builds, remote-friendly across time zones.
